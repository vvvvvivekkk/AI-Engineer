# Customer Support

## How to contact us

- Live chat on brightleaf.example, every day 8:00 to 22:00
- Email support@brightleaf.example, answered within 24 hours
- Phone, Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:00

## Escalations

If you are not happy with the first answer, ask for a team leader. Team leaders reply within 2 business days. Complaints that remain unresolved can be sent to the independent retail ombudsman.

## Response targets

We aim to answer 90% of chats within 2 minutes and 95% of emails within 24 hours.

## Accessibility

Our website follows WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines. Customers who need help placing an order can call our accessibility line and an agent will place the order with them.
