# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Brightleaf gift cards. We do not accept cheques or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order is dispatched. For pre-orders, you are charged when the item ships.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry it once automatically after 10 minutes. If it fails again, the order is cancelled and you receive an email. You are never charged twice for the same order. Temporary holds from failed attempts are released by your bank, usually within 3 business days.

## Instalments

Orders over $150 can be paid in 4 interest-free instalments through our payment partner. A credit check may apply.

## Invoices

A VAT invoice is attached to your dispatch email. You can also download invoices from the **Orders** page for up to 7 years.
