# Price Match Promise

If you find the same product cheaper at another retailer, we match the price.

## Conditions

- The product must be identical: same brand, model, size and colour.
- The competitor must be a registered retailer that ships to your country, with the item in stock.
- The competitor price must include delivery costs.
- Marketplace sellers, auction sites and member-only prices are excluded.

## Time limit

You can request a price match before you buy or within **14 days after** your purchase.

## How to request

Contact support or ask in store, with a link to the competitor's page. If approved, we refund the difference to your original payment method.

## Exclusions

The promise does not apply to clearance items, bundles, or prices during Black Friday week.
