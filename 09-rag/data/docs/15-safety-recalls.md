# Product Safety and Recalls

## How we handle recalls

If we find a safety problem with a product, we stop selling it immediately, publish a notice on the **Safety notices** page and email every customer who bought it.

## Current recall: TrailGlow 300 headlamp

Some TrailGlow 300 headlamps sold between March and June 2026 can overheat while charging. Stop using the headlamp and return it to any store or by free post for a **full refund, even without a receipt**. Serial numbers starting with TG3-26A are affected.

## Reporting a safety concern

If you think a product is unsafe, stop using it and report it through the **Safety** form or by phone. Our safety team responds within 1 business day.

## Fuel and stove safety

Never use gas stoves inside a tent. Store fuel canisters below 40 degrees Celsius and away from children.
