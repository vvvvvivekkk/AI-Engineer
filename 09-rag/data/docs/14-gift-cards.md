# Gift Cards

## Buying gift cards

Digital gift cards are available from $10 to $500 and are delivered by email within 1 hour. Physical gift cards can be bought in any store.

## Validity

Gift cards are valid for **3 years** from the date of purchase. The expiry date is printed on the card and shown in the email.

## Using gift cards

Gift cards can be used online and in stores, and you can combine up to 3 gift cards in one order. They cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Lost cards

Lost digital cards can be resent if you have the order number. Lost physical cards cannot be replaced unless they were registered to your account.
