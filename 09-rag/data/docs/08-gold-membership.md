# Brightleaf Gold Membership

Brightleaf Gold is our paid membership for frequent adventurers.

## Price

Gold costs **$49 per year**. Membership renews automatically; you can cancel at any time from your account and keep the benefits until the end of the paid year.

## Benefits

- Free express delivery on all orders
- Extended return and exchange window of 60 days
- 10% off rentals
- Early access to sales, 24 hours before everyone else
- One free jacket re-proofing per year at the repair workshop

## Points

Gold members earn 2 points per dollar spent (standard members earn 1). 100 points are worth $1 of store credit. Points expire 24 months after they are earned.

## Refunds of the membership fee

If you cancel within 14 days of joining and have not used any benefit, we refund the $49 in full.
