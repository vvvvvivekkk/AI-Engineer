# Returns Policy

Brightleaf Outdoor Gear wants every customer to be happy with their purchase. This policy explains how returns work for orders placed on brightleaf.example and in our three stores.

## Return window

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery**. For orders collected in store, the 30 days start on the collection date. Brightleaf Gold members get an extended return window of **60 days**.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused, clean and in their original packaging with all tags attached. Footwear must not have been worn outdoors. We inspect every return and may refuse items that show signs of use.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Items bought in the clearance sale (marked "Final Sale")
- Opened fuel canisters and gas cartridges
- Personalised or engraved products
- Gift cards

## How to start a return

Log in to your account, open **Orders**, choose the item and click **Start a return**. You will receive a prepaid return label by email within one business day. Return shipping is free for customers in the mainland; customers on islands pay a flat fee of $6.

## Refund timing

Once the parcel reaches our warehouse, we inspect it within 3 business days. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.
