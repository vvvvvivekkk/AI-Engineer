# Gear Rental

Try before you buy, or rent for a single trip.

## What you can rent

Tents, sleeping bags, backpacks, trekking poles, snowshoes and climbing helmets. Rentals are available at the Riverside and Hillcrest stores only.

## Prices and deposit

Rental prices are listed per day, with a weekend rate covering Friday to Monday. A refundable deposit of **$100** is charged for each rental order and released when the gear comes back undamaged.

## Late returns

Late returns are charged at the daily rate for each extra day. After 7 days late, the item is treated as sold and the full retail price is charged, minus the deposit.

## Cleaning and damage

Please return gear dry. A cleaning fee of $20 applies to very dirty items. Damage beyond normal wear is charged at the repair cost.

## Rent-to-own

If you buy the same product within 30 days of renting it, we deduct the rental fee from the purchase price.
