# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery options

| Option | Time | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | $4.95, free on orders over $75 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $12.95 |
| Store collection | Ready in 2 hours | Free |

Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day are dispatched the same day.

## Island and remote deliveries

Deliveries to islands and remote areas take 2 extra business days. Express delivery is not available for these addresses.

## Tracking your order

You receive a tracking link by email and SMS when your parcel leaves our warehouse. Tracking can take up to 12 hours to show the first scan.

## Missing or late parcels

If your parcel has not arrived 5 business days after the latest estimated date, contact support. We open an investigation with the courier, which takes up to 7 business days. If the parcel is confirmed lost, we send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Damaged on arrival

Take photos of the box and the item and report the damage within **48 hours** of delivery through the **Orders** page. Do not throw away the packaging until the claim is closed.
