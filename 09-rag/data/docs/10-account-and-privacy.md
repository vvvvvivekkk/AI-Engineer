# Account and Privacy

## Creating an account

You need an email address and a password of at least 10 characters. You can also sign in with Google or Apple.

## Password reset

Click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. The reset email arrives within 10 minutes and the link is valid for 1 hour. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder, then contact support.

## Changing your email address

Go to **Account settings > Profile**, enter the new address and confirm it from the verification email. Your order history stays linked to your account.

## Your data

We store your name, addresses, orders and preferences to run your account. We never sell personal data. You can download a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account from **Account settings > Privacy**. Deletion is completed within 30 days; invoices are kept for 7 years because the law requires it.

## Marketing emails

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with one click at the bottom of any email. Order and delivery emails cannot be turned off.
