# Repair Service

Repairing gear is better for the planet than replacing it. Brightleaf runs a repair workshop at the Riverside store.

## What we repair

We repair zips, seams, tent poles, buckles and small tears in fabric. We also re-proof waterproof jackets. We do not repair electronics or footwear soles.

## Prices

Repairs covered by warranty are free. Other repairs are priced after inspection. A typical zip replacement on a jacket costs $25, and a tent pole section replacement costs $15.

## Turnaround time

Most repairs take 10 to 15 business days. In the busy season (May to August) repairs can take up to 4 weeks.

## How to send an item for repair

Book a repair online, print the repair form and either drop the item at any store or post it to the Riverside workshop. Postage for repairs is paid by the customer unless the repair is covered by warranty.
