# Stores and Opening Hours

Brightleaf has three stores.

## Riverside (flagship)

Open Monday to Saturday 9:00 to 20:00 and Sunday 10:00 to 17:00. Home of the repair workshop and the rental desk. Free parking for 2 hours.

## Hillcrest

Open Monday to Saturday 10:00 to 19:00, closed on Sundays. Has a rental desk and a climbing-shoe fitting area.

## Harbour Market

Open every day 10:00 to 18:00. A smaller store focused on clothing and footwear. No rentals and no repairs.

## Public holidays

All stores open 10:00 to 16:00 on public holidays, except New Year's Day and Christmas Day, when they are closed.
