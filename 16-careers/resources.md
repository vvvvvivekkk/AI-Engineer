# Module 16 — Resources

Curated from the course's source catalogues (`source-notes/16-careers.md` and `source-notes/12-ai-trainer-jobs.md`), plus a few well-known official sites for job boards and salary research. Platforms, programmes and pay change often — as of late 2026, always check the official page.

**Referral-link policy:** the AI-trainer aggregator below earns referral fees from platform sign-ups. We link only to its guide and listing pages, never to referral links. Find each platform's official site yourself and apply there.

## Understanding the roles

| Resource | Why it is useful |
|---|---|
| [The Rise of the AI Engineer (Latent Space)](https://www.latent.space/p/ai-engineer) | Explains what the AI-engineer job is and how it differs from ML engineering. |
| [Latent Space podcast](https://www.latent.space/podcast) | Keeps you current on tools and hiring trends in AI engineering. |
| [Latent Space 2025 AI Engineering Reading List](https://www.latent.space/p/2025-papers) | The shared reading list many interviewers assume you know. |
| [AI Engineer World's Fair talks](https://www.ai.engineer/worldsfair) | Talks by practitioners at companies that hire AI engineers. |
| [How to Interview and Hire ML/AI Engineers (Eugene Yan)](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/how-to-interview/?utm_source=pocket_saves) | The hiring side's view, so you know what is being assessed. |

## Job boards and internships

| Resource | Why it is useful |
|---|---|
| [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com) | The main job and networking platform; contacting hiring managers directly works. |
| [Internshala](https://internshala.com) | Large Indian internship portal with many AI/ML and GenAI roles. |
