# Short cover letter / application note template

Many applications only allow a short note. Keep it under 200 words and specific.

> Dear [Hiring Manager name, or "Hiring Team"],
>
> I'm applying for the [Role] position at [Company]. [One sentence on why this company specifically - a product, mission, blog post or recent launch.]
>
> In my recent project [Project], I [built what] using [relevant tech from the JD], which [result with a number]. I also [second proof: internship, AI-trainer work, hackathon, open source], where I [relevant skill].
>
> From the job description, I understand you need someone who can [top 2 requirements]. I'd bring [how your experience matches] and I'm keen to learn [something genuine about their stack].
>
> My portfolio is at [link] and my GitHub at [link]. Thank you for your time - I'd welcome the chance to discuss how I can help [team/goal].
>
> Kind regards,
> [Name] · [email] · [phone]
