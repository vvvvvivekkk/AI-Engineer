# Outreach message templates

Rules: short (under ~120 words), specific to the person, one small ask, no attachment in the first message, polite follow-up once after about a week, then stop.

## 1. Connection request (LinkedIn has a short character limit)
> Hi [Name], I enjoyed your post on [specific topic]. I'm building [your project] and learning [area]. Would love to connect and follow your work. — [Your name]

## 2. Asking an engineer for a 15-minute chat
> Hi [Name], I'm a [student/junior developer] focused on [LLM apps]. I saw that your team at [Company] works on [product/feature] — I especially liked [specific detail]. I recently built [project + one result] ([link]).
> Would you be open to a 15-minute call sometime in the next two weeks? I'd love to hear how your team approaches [specific question]. Totally fine if you're busy.
> Thanks, [Name]

## 3. Message to a hiring manager about an open role
> Hi [Name], I applied for the [role] position (ref [ID if any]). I think I'd be a strong fit because I've already built [relevant project] doing [similar thing to the JD], achieving [result] ([link]).
> If helpful, I'd be glad to walk you through it in 10 minutes. Thank you for considering my application.
> [Name] · [LinkedIn] · [GitHub]

## 4. Asking for a referral (only after a real conversation)
> Hi [Name], thanks again for the chat last week about [topic] — your advice on [x] was really useful, and I've since [what you did with it].
> I noticed [Company] has a [role] opening ([link]). Based on what you told me about the team, I think my experience with [skills/project] fits well. Would you be comfortable referring me? I've attached my resume and can send a short summary you could forward. No pressure at all if not.

## 5. Follow-up after an interview
> Hi [Name], thank you for your time today. I enjoyed discussing [specific topic], and it made me even more excited about [team/product]. [Optional: one-line extra thought or link related to something discussed.] Looking forward to hearing about next steps.

## 6. Freelance / small-business pitch
> Hi [Name], I noticed [specific observation about their business, e.g. many customer questions on Instagram go unanswered]. I build small AI assistants that [concrete outcome]. Here is a 1-minute demo I made for a similar business: [link]. Would a short call next week be useful?
