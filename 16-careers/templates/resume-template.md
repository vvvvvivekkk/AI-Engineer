## Bullet formula and strong verbs

**Action verb + what + how + result.** Example: "Built a RAG assistant over 1,200 policy pages (FastAPI, FAISS, BM25); raised retrieval hit rate from 0.71 to 0.89 on a 60-question eval set."

Verbs: built, designed, deployed, automated, evaluated, reduced, improved, optimised, fine-tuned, integrated, led, migrated, shipped, taught, analysed, documented.

Numbers you can use without a job: accuracy, hit rate, latency (p95), cost per request, documents indexed, users, test coverage, time saved, model size, dataset size.
