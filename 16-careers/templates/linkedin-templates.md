# LinkedIn templates

Replace every `[bracket]`. Keep your headline under about 220 characters and front-load the keywords recruiters search for.

## Headline formulas

1. `[Role] | [Focus 1], [Focus 2], [Focus 3] | [Proof]`
   - e.g. `AI Engineer | RAG, Agents, LLMOps | Built 3 deployed LLM apps`
2. `[Student/Graduate] → [Target role] | [Focus] | [Achievement]`
   - e.g. `CS student → AI Engineer | LLM apps & evaluation | Hackathon finalist`
3. `[Role] helping [who] [do what] with [how]`
   - e.g. `AI Trainer & Python developer helping AI labs evaluate code models`

## About section (3 short paragraphs)

> I build [what] for [who/what problem]. Recently I [best project + result], and [second proof].
>
> I work mostly with [5-8 keywords: Python, FastAPI, RAG, LangGraph, Docker, evals, ...]. I care about [an engineering value: measuring quality, shipping reliable systems, clear docs].
>
> I'm currently looking for [role type, e.g. junior AI engineer / internship] roles [location/remote]. Happy to chat about [topics]. [Email or "DM me"].

## Featured section
Pin: (1) your best project demo or video, (2) a blog post explaining a technical lesson, (3) your GitHub profile.
